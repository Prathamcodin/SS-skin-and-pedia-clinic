Frequently Asked Questions

You can book through our website form (sent via WhatsApp), call +91 62638 95213, or message us on WhatsApp. We confirm your slot by call or WhatsApp with the doctor's available timing.

Walk-ins are welcome subject to availability. Appointments are recommended for shorter waiting times, especially for pediatric vaccinations and aesthetic procedures.

Consultation fees vary by department and type of visit (first follow-up, procedure). Please call or WhatsApp the clinic for current dermatology and pediatrics consultation rates before your visit.

Pediatrics (Dr. Siddharth): Mon–Sat 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM & 4:00 PM – 7:30 PM. Dermatology (Dr. Swati): Mon–Sat 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM & 4:00 PM – 7:30 PM. Sunday visits are by appointment only.

We provide scheduled immunizations per IAP guidelines including optional vaccines. Bring your child's vaccination card; our team will counsel you on due doses and any mild post-vaccine care.

Medical dermatology for acne, pigmentation, hair fall, allergies, and infections. Aesthetic options include chemical peels, microneedling, PRP, and laser-based treatments after consultation with Dr. Swati Gupta.

We primarily operate on direct payment (cash, UPI, and cards where available). For insurance reimbursement, please check with your provider; we can provide consultation bills and prescriptions as required.

Carry a valid ID, prior prescriptions or reports, allergy list, and for children — vaccination card and growth records if any. For skin concerns, note products or medicines you currently use.
